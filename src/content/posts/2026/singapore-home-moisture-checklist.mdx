Before buying a dehumidifier, find out where the water is coming from. A leaking window, damp cabinet and bathroom that stays wet after use are different problems. Recording when and where moisture appears gives you a better starting point than choosing an appliance by its advertised capacity alone.

November is a useful time to prepare. Meteorological Service Singapore describes the Northeast Monsoon as generally running from December to early March; November belongs to the preceding inter-monsoon period. Singapore has rain and high humidity throughout the year, so this is a seasonal check, not a suggestion that moisture matters only in December. [MSS climate overview](https://www.weather.gov.sg/climate-climate-of-singapore/).

## Start with a room-by-room record

</figure>

Walk through the home after rain and again after ordinary activities such as showering or drying laundry. Note visible water, condensation, musty smells and discolouration without assuming that all stains are mould. Photograph changes and record the date. A repeated wet patch near a window calls for a different conversation from damp clothes put away before they were dry.

Use a simple log:

| Location | What you can observe | Next question |
|---|---|---|
| Window or external wall | Water appearing after rain | Does a seal, drain or building defect need inspection? |
| Bathroom | Surfaces remaining wet after use | Is extraction working and where does it discharge? |
| Wardrobe | Damp items or recurring odour | Are clothes fully dry before storage; is there a nearby moisture source? |
| Laundry area | Condensation during indoor drying | Can the drying arrangement or moisture removal be improved? |

These are prompts for investigation, not diagnoses. If you rent, send a concise dated record to the owner or managing agent. Avoid covering a recurring patch with paint before the cause has been assessed.

## Treat the cause before the symptom

The US Environmental Protection Agency makes moisture control central to mould prevention. Its household guidance recommends fixing water problems and drying wet materials promptly, generally within 24–48 hours after water damage. It also distinguishes small clean-ups from situations that need professional help, including contaminated water or extensive damage. These are US household guidelines, not a substitute for assessing a particular Singapore property. [EPA household guide](https://www.epa.gov/mold/brief-guide-mold-moisture-and-your-home).

An appliance cannot repair a leak. Nor does a pleasant smell show that damp material is dry. Keep a record of what has been repaired and whether the same conditions return. For a persistent problem, that history may be more useful to a contractor than a single photograph taken after the surface has been wiped.

Do not identify a mould species from its colour or use this checklist to judge health effects. If a household member has symptoms or a condition that makes exposure a concern, seek appropriate medical advice and arrange a qualified assessment of the building problem.

## How to choose a dehumidifier: measure first

<figure>
<Image src={media3} alt="A generic digital humidity meter with a blank screen beside a notebook." loading="lazy" />
<figcaption>AI-generated conceptual illustration; no actual home inspection, appliance test or measured result is shown. Credit: Arahkaii / AI-generated illustration.</figcaption>
</figure>

A humidity meter can help you observe a pattern. Place it away from direct splashes and record the location and time alongside the reading. Compare similar conditions rather than treating readings from different corners of the home as interchangeable. Check the device’s instructions and accuracy specification; a single low-cost reading is not a building survey.

If you are comparing dehumidifiers, ask for the conditions under which each quoted extraction rate was measured. A litres-per-day figure without its test temperature and humidity is an incomplete comparison. Also record the stated power input, tank capacity, drainage options, sound rating and clearance requirements. These questions do not establish which model is best, but they help you avoid comparing unlike specifications.

Think about where the appliance will stand, how water will be emptied and whether a safe drainage route exists. Check the manufacturer’s instructions before buying for a bathroom or other wet area. A compact appliance that cannot be positioned or maintained as directed is not a useful solution for that space.

## A local comparison: the small print changes the calculation

We compared two specification tables on Novita’s Singapore store on 25 October 2026. These are manufacturer-stated figures, not our measurements or a recommendation to buy either model.

| Published specification | ND298 | ND30 |
|---|---|---|
| Extraction rate | 12 litres per day at 30°C and 80% relative humidity | 30 litres per day at 30°C and 80% relative humidity |
| Stated operating power | 165 watts at 27°C and 60% relative humidity | 490 watts at 30°C and 80% relative humidity |
| Tank capacity | 2.6 litres | 3.8 litres |
| Continuous drainage | Listed as available | Listed as available |

Sources: the manufacturer’s [ND298 specifications](https://estore.novita.com.sg/products/dehumidifier-nd298) and [ND30 specifications](https://estore.novita.com.sg/products/dehumidifier-nd30). We selected these two pages because both identify the conditions behind their figures. This is a specification-reading example, not a survey of the market; prices and stock are excluded.

The extraction figures share a stated test condition, but the power figures do not. Dividing each extraction figure by its headline wattage would therefore combine mismatched conditions for the ND298. That calculation cannot fairly establish an efficiency winner. Ask for energy-use and extraction data measured under the same conditions before making that comparison.

Tank capacity answers a different question from extraction capacity. It tells you how much the container holds, not how much moisture the machine will remove from your room in a day. Both pages offer continuous drainage, but that does not establish whether your intended position has a suitable route. Check the exact model’s installation instructions and inspect the space before ordering.

## Make a realistic running-cost estimate

<figure>
<Image src={media5} alt="A blank calculator, timer and notebook on a pale tabletop." loading="lazy" />
<figcaption>AI-generated conceptual illustration; no actual home inspection, appliance test or measured result is shown. Credit: Arahkaii / AI-generated illustration.</figcaption>

Use the model’s rated power and your electricity tariff to make a clearly labelled estimate. For example, a hypothetical 300-watt appliance operating continuously at that input for eight hours would use 2.4 kilowatt-hours. Multiply 2.4 by your tariff per kilowatt-hour. Actual consumption can differ because equipment cycles and operating conditions vary; this is not a measured cost for a real product.

The same discipline applies to noise. A quoted sound figure may help shortlist equipment, but ask how it was measured and whether it refers to the mode you intend to use. If bedroom use is important, check the return conditions and whether a demonstration is available. This comparison does not rank the models; no appliance testing was conducted.

## Build a repeatable routine

<figure>
<Image src={media6} alt="Folded towels in an open wooden cupboard beside a notebook on a table." loading="lazy" />
<figcaption>AI-generated conceptual illustration; no actual home inspection, appliance test or measured result is shown. Credit: Arahkaii / AI-generated illustration.</figcaption>
</figure>

Choose a manageable inspection rhythm: after heavy rain, after a leak repair and whenever a familiar patch returns. Empty and clean equipment according to its manual. Keep the maintenance instructions somewhere everyone using the appliance can find them.

For valuable paper pieces, use a more specific conservation approach rather than assuming a room appliance solves every storage problem. Arahkaii’s [guide to displaying art on paper in a humid home](https://www.arahkaii.com/living/display-art-on-paper-humid-home/) discusses the questions to take to a framer.

A useful moisture plan is a record of causes, actions and results. Start with the building, observe what changes and buy equipment for a defined job.
