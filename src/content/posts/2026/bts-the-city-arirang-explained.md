In Seoul in March, Sungnyemun was lit up with BTS visuals. Sungnyemun is the 14th-century gate that was once the city's grand southern entrance. When organisers later counted who came to see it, about seven in ten visitors were foreign nationals. It's a small number, but it explains the whole idea. A concert lasts around three hours and only ticket holders get in. A city lasts as long as you can keep it lit, and anyone can walk into it.

That's what BTS THE CITY ARIRANG is: everything around the concert, put across a whole city. On 1 December it comes to Singapore for its sixth and final run of 2026\.

---

## **So what exactly is BTS THE CITY?**

BTS THE CITY is HYBE's format for **extending a concert into its host city**. HYBE is BTS's parent company, and it works with each city's tourism board or main local partners. While BTS is in town for the shows, the city itself becomes part of the event: landmarks are lit in the album's colour, installations go up in public spaces, and transport, hotels, restaurants and shops all join in. Fans follow it all with a stamp rally that takes them from one stop to the next.

It has three working parts:

1. **Light.** Landmarks change colour together. For the *ARIRANG* era the colour is a deep red, which Singapore's programme calls "Ari Red". That shared colour turns a skyline into a sign anyone can recognise, and a photo of it travels around the world within an hour.  
2. **Place.** Installations, exhibitions and pop-ups are matched to each city's own landmarks: a royal gate in Seoul, the Strip in Las Vegas, an indoor waterfall in Singapore. The local stops stand out against the BTS branding everywhere else.  
3. **Movement.** The stamp rally, themed transport cards, wrapped buses and trains, and bicycles. The project is designed so you see the city in order to complete it.

The important thing is that **most of it is free and you don't need a ticket**. The concerts sell out in minutes. THE CITY is how the other 99% of the fanbase gets to take part.

## **Where did BTS THE CITY start?**

The idea isn't new to the *ARIRANG* era. It began in **Las Vegas in 2022**, during the *Permission to Dance on Stage* shows at Allegiant Stadium, when the Strip was lit purple and the city put on BTS-themed exhibitions and installations. Even then it was unusual. Local tourism officials said the only other events to get a city-wide activation like that were the Super Bowl and Formula 1\.

For 2026 HYBE rebuilt the format at a much bigger scale and took it on tour. Korean press have called this version "Global THE CITY 2.0".

## **Where has BTS THE CITY ARIRANG run so far?**

### **Seoul, from 20 March**

It started on the day *ARIRANG* came out. Sungnyemun and N Seoul Tower carried BTS visuals, a drone show lit up the sky over Ttukseom Hangang Park, Dongdaemun Design Plaza and Banpo Bridge put on music light shows, and a special Seoul City Tour Bus route linked Gwanghwamun, Namdaemun Market, HYBE's headquarters and Gyeongbokgung. Two figures show the effect. More than 86% of visitors to the fan space at DDP came from overseas, and the city tour bus carried about a fifth more passengers than usual.

### **Las Vegas, 20–31 May**

The format came back to where it began, now in *ARIRANG* red. The Strip and its digital marquees changed colour, and the Sphere ran a BTS activation, around four nights at Allegiant Stadium.

### **Busan, 5–21 June**

Busan made itself the main stage. Two concerts at Busan Asiad Main Stadium drew about 110,000 people, and a thousand-drone show over Gwangalli Beach drew the faces of all seven members against the sea.

### **London, July**

This was the first edition in Europe, timed to two nights at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium on 6 and 7 July. The London Eye went red for the occasion.

### **New York, 24 July–3 August**

Built around two shows at MetLife Stadium, the New York edition took over Vanderbilt Hall at Grand Central, Top of the Rock, the Korean Cultural Center New York and the Arte Museum. It added a BTS city bus tour and a stamp rally across Manhattan and beyond.

### **Singapore, 1–31 December**

The sixth host city, the first in Southeast Asia, the last stop of 2026 and, at a full month, **the longest edition so far**.

## **Why is Singapore the finale?**

Partly timing. The Singapore concerts on 17, 19, 20 and 22 December come at the very end of the year, and the tour continues into 2027\. But three other things made Singapore a natural place to finish.

**It gets the most concerts of any city in the Asia leg.** Four National Stadium nights gave THE CITY a long enough concert week to justify building a whole month around it. The New York and Las Vegas editions lasted under two weeks. Singapore runs for 31 days.

**Singapore has run this kind of project before.** The Singapore Tourism Board is behind THE CITY Singapore together with HYBE. The board has spent the past few years treating stadium tours as tourism projects in their own right, and the 2024 Eras Tour and its Southeast Asia exclusivity is the best-known example. THE CITY suits that approach: it gives visiting fans reasons to stay longer, spend more time at attractions and see more of the island than the stadium. (We'll look at the strategy in more detail in an upcoming piece on why Singapore won the takeover.)

**The city is small and well connected.** Korean coverage of the announcement focused on a "seamless experience" linking the airport, landmarks, transport and hotels, and Singapore is built for that. A fan can land at Changi, see the Rain Vortex show in red, check in near Marina Bay and walk the lit bridges before bed, all on the first night. Few host cities can promise that.

## **What makes the Singapore edition different?**

Each ‘THE CITY’ has used its host's character. Singapore's version uses three things the island does well.

**Water and light.** Jewel's Rain Vortex is the world's tallest indoor waterfall, and for 26 nights the BTS show will play on it. Add the Marina Bay skyline, the Supertrees and a drone show over Palawan Beach, and Singapore's edition is mostly a series of nights.

**Culture alongside spectacle.** The National Gallery has built a ticketed Art Trail pairing BTS songs with works from its *Pop after Pop: Art around Us* exhibition. The National Museum has an outdoor Dance Mirror, a façade light-up and a random-play dance night. Asking the national cultural institutions to take part instead of only the malls is a notable choice, and it fits an album named after a folk song.

**Local brands, not only global ones.** New Bahru, the creative hub on Kim Yam Road, hosts a week of BTS-themed products and menus from Singapore's independent labels. There are BTS SimplyGo EZ-Link cards and branded HelloRide bicycles. Global partners are in the mix, but the souvenirs fans take home will often be made in Singapore.

## **Why are pop tours becoming tourism events?**

For a city, a sold-out stadium concert has a ceiling: the number of seats. THE CITY removes that limit. The Seoul figures show what happens when you do. The fans who came weren't only ticket holders. Most were travellers, many of them without tickets, there for the atmosphere. A tourism board can work with that: the extra hotel nights, the trips to attractions and the photos of a red Merlion posted at midnight.

For fans, the deal is different but just as real. A ticket is a lottery. The city is open to everyone. A teenager in Tampines whose parents couldn't get seats can still stand under the Rain Vortex at 8pm on 1 December, hear the opening notes of the album, and feel part of the night.

That openness is also what makes it worth covering for a publication like ours. Fan events often lean on nightlife, and THE CITY Singapore has its share, which we leave out. But the core of the format is public, free and family-friendly: light, art, walking routes and a city to explore. That's the version we'll cover.

## **Frequently asked questions**

**What is BTS THE CITY ARIRANG?** It's HYBE's city-wide fan experience that runs alongside BTS's *ARIRANG* world tour. Each host city lights its landmarks in the album's red and hosts installations, exhibitions, light shows and themed transport and retail. Most of it is free.

**Which cities have hosted BTS THE CITY ARIRANG?** Seoul (from 20 March 2026), Las Vegas (20–31 May), Busan (5–21 June), London (July), New York (24 July–3 August) and Singapore (1–31 December). The format first appeared in Las Vegas in 2022\.

**Who organises BTS THE CITY Singapore?** HYBE together with the Singapore Tourism Board, with partners including Jewel Changi Airport, the National Gallery, the National Museum, Sentosa, Resorts World Sentosa, Mandai Wildlife Reserve and The Kallang.

**Do I need a concert ticket to join BTS THE CITY?** No. Most activations, including the Jewel light show, the red landmarks and the National Museum installations, are free and open to everyone.

**How long does BTS THE CITY Singapore last?** The whole month, from 1 to 31 December 2026\. That makes it the longest edition so far.
