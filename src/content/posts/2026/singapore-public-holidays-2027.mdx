Singapore's 2027 public-holiday calendar gives a conventional Monday-to-Friday worker five three-day weekends without annual leave. The most flexible longer break sits in May: Hari Raya Haji on Monday 17 May and Vesak Day on Thursday 20 May can be joined with three working days of leave to make nine consecutive days off.

Those are calendar calculations, not a guarantee of time off for every employee. They assume Saturday and Sunday are non-working days, no holiday substitution applies, and the employee is free on the gazetted holidays. Shift patterns, employer arrangements and individual entitlements need checking before a booking.

## The official dates

The [Ministry of Manpower's release of 18 June 2026](https://www.mom.gov.sg/newsroom/press-releases/2026/0618-public-holidays-for-2027) lists the following 11 gazetted holidays. Monday 8 February is also a public holiday following Chinese New Year on Sunday 7 February.

| Holiday | Date in 2027 | Weekday |
|---|---|---|
| New Year's Day | 1 January | Friday |
| Chinese New Year | 6 and 7 February | Saturday and Sunday |
| Additional public holiday following Sunday CNY | 8 February | Monday |
| Hari Raya Puasa | 10 March | Wednesday |
| Good Friday | 26 March | Friday |
| Labour Day | 1 May | Saturday |
| Hari Raya Haji | 17 May | Monday |
| Vesak Day | 20 May | Thursday |
| National Day | 9 August | Monday |
| Deepavali | 28 October | Thursday |
| Christmas Day | 25 December | Saturday |

The table separates the additional Monday for clarity; it is not an extra named festival. A Saturday holiday is not automatically a Monday public holiday. For employment entitlements, consult [MOM's public-holiday guidance](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/public-holidays) and your employer rather than treating a travel calendar as payroll advice.

For the working pattern described above, the uninterrupted three-day breaks are 1–3 January, 6–8 February, 26–28 March, 15–17 May and 7–9 August. This count includes the February Monday explicitly identified by MOM and does not invent a Monday substitution for Labour Day or Christmas.

A three-day break usually gives a traveller two nights if departure and return both fall inside it. That arithmetic matters more than an attractive flight duration. A late arrival on Friday and early return on Sunday leave one usable day; an awkward transfer can consume much of that.

Compare the complete door-to-door journey before choosing a destination. Note when you leave home, when you expect to reach the room, when you must leave it and whether meals or activities are possible on the partial days. Do the same for a holiday in Singapore. The latter may produce more usable hours even if its room rate looks less attractive in isolation.

| Leave to request | Consecutive break | Days off | Annual-leave days |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Friday 21 May | 20–23 May | 4 | 1 |
| Friday 29 October | 28–31 October | 4 | 1 |

Both rows are simple Thursday-holiday extensions. Check the exact dates against your work roster before requesting leave.

For a cleaner four-day New Year trip, Thursday 31 December **2026** can join 1–3 January **2027**, using one day from the applicable 2026 leave arrangement. That crosses calendar years; whether leave can be used or carried over is an employer question.

Hari Raya Puasa falls midweek. Taking Monday 8 and Tuesday 9 March creates five consecutive days from 6–10 March. Taking Thursday 11 and Friday 12 March instead creates 10–14 March. Both options use two leave days under our stated workweek assumption. Choose around family commitments before choosing around airfare.

## May: three days of leave, nine consecutive days

<figure>
<Image src={media3} alt="15–23 May 2027, with 18, 19 and 21 marked as leave days." loading="lazy" />
<figcaption>AI-generated illustration of the verified May leave calculation: three leave days make nine consecutive days off under the stated workweek assumptions. Credit: Arahkaii / AI-generated illustration.</figcaption>
</figure>

Take Tuesday 18, Wednesday 19 and Friday 21 May. Combined with the two public holidays and the weekends on either side, the break runs from Saturday 15 to Sunday 23 May inclusive: nine days, of which three are annual leave.

This is not the same as “nine free holiday days”. The three leave days have an opportunity cost, and approval may be competitive. Nor does the favourable calendar imply a cheap trip. No 2027 fare or hotel-price dataset was collected for this article.

The extra time can be used in two different ways. One is to travel further. The other is to keep the destination simple and spend longer there. For a family, the second may be the more useful luxury: arrival day can remain an arrival day, and an activity can move when someone needs rest.

Before applying for leave, sketch the nine-day version and a shorter four-day version around Vesak Day. Compare the extra accommodation and leave cost against the experiences those additional days actually buy. If the longer plan merely adds transfers, it may not be the better holiday.

## Where to go: four bases, matched to the available days

The shortlist below is for a cultural break, with one accommodation base and an identifiable museum anchor. These are editorial choices based on the length of each break and published visitor information—not claims about the best weather, cheapest flights or confirmed 2027 opening. A two-night overseas option only works if the actual transport leaves enough time to use it.

| Holiday or combination | Starting choice | Why it fits—and what could rule it out |
|---|---|---|
| New Year, 1–3 January | Singapore | Make the short break about an art day rather than a transfer. Check the Gallery's actual holiday programme. |
| CNY, 6–8 February | Singapore | Retain flexibility around family commitments. Do not assume an overseas museum will open during the same festival. |
| Puasa, either five-day leave bridge | Kuching | Four nights allow a museum day plus unscheduled time. Confirm the museum's 2027 Eid closure before booking. |
| Good Friday, 26–28 March | George Town | A two-night cultural visit is an option if connections work; the Farquhar museum's regular Friday closure makes Saturday the provisional museum day. |
| Labour Day, 1–2 May | Singapore | This is an ordinary two-day weekend under our assumptions. Keep the journey short rather than invent an extra Monday. |
| Haji, 15–17 May | Kuching | A shorter version of the museum-led trip, conditional on transport and the local holiday opening notice. |
| Vesak, 20–23 May with one leave day | George Town | Three nights give flexibility around the Farquhar museum's Friday closure; verify Thursday or weekend opening. |
| Combined May break, 15–23 May | Tokyo | Use the longer window for a single-base art-and-museum trip, with arrival and departure days left light. |
| National Day, 7–9 August | Kuching | Another two-night candidate if the flight times work; check the destination's own calendar, not Singapore's alone. |
| Deepavali, 28–31 October with one leave day | George Town | The four-day shape again lets the museum day move away from Friday. No weather or crowd advantage is implied. |
| Christmas, 25–26 December | Singapore | Another two-day weekend. Choose a local programme after checking Christmas opening; no Monday holiday is assumed. |

**Singapore: choose the display before the room.** [National Gallery Singapore's visitor information](https://www.nationalgallery.sg/sg/en/visit/visitor-information.html) flags the UOB Southeast Asia Gallery's closure until late 2027, while works remain elsewhere in the building. Its non-ticketed Keppel Centre for Art Education is a family option, but specific programmes need their own checks. A hotel is optional: the decision is whether an overnight stay adds enough to a cultural day you could also take from home.

**Kuching: give the museum a proper morning.** The [Borneo Cultures Museum](https://museum.sarawak.gov.my/web/subpage/webpage_view/169%20) is in central Kuching beside Padang Merdeka. Its children's river-themed gallery and galleries on nature, history and objects provide different starting points for mixed-age groups. Current published last admission is 4pm, with different weekday and weekend opening times. That makes it a poor activity to squeeze after an uncertain late arrival. The closure list available at our check covers 2026; it cannot establish 2027 Eid or other holiday access. Put the museum on a full day only after that notice is checked.

**George Town: Friday is the useful constraint.** The [Penang State Museum's Farquhar announcement](https://penangmuseum.gov.my/relaunch-farquhar/) identifies the visitor site at 1 Lebuh Farquhar and lists 9am–5pm opening, except Fridays and named festival closures. This is why the Good Friday break needs a Saturday museum plan, and why the Thursday-to-Sunday breaks offer more room to rearrange. Treat those as provisional applications of the present weekly pattern, not confirmed 2027 reservations. Do not confuse the Farquhar visitor site with the museum body's separate administration address.

**Tokyo: spend the extra days on flexibility.** [Tokyo National Museum's visitor page](https://www.tnm.jp/modules/r_free_page/index.php?id=113&lang=en) lists Japanese, Asian and other collections, a regular Monday closure with holiday exceptions, and individual room closures. For the May break, provisionally reserve a midweek museum day, then verify the dated calendar before fixing it. Leave the adjacent day open for a second visit or a different interest rather than automatically adding another city. The [museum guidelines](https://www.tnm.jp/modules/r_free_page/index.php?id=127) also prohibit bringing suitcases into the museum: plan luggage storage separately from a museum visit on departure day. This is a cultural starting point for a nine-day Tokyo stay, not a fully researched nine-day itinerary or a forecast of May conditions.

## School holidays are a separate calendar

Do not assume that a workplace long weekend makes a longer trip possible for children. This article verifies Singapore's public holidays, not the 2027 school terms, individual school commitments or examination dates. Cross-check the current [MOE calendar](https://www.moe.gov.sg/calendar) and your child's school before requesting leave for a family trip.

The same applies to the destination's calendar. A Singapore public holiday can coincide with local closures, festivals or another country's holiday. Price comparisons should use the exact dates and the same cancellation terms; comparisons between unlike rooms and fares can make an inconvenient itinerary look like a saving.
