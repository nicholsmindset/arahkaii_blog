At 8pm on 1 December, the 40-metre waterfall at Jewel Changi Airport will turn red. That's the starting signal. For the next 31 days Singapore becomes the last stop of BTS's 2026: the Rain Vortex plays the *ARIRANG* album, Marina Bay and the river bridges glow red at night, a ten-metre inflatable sits on Palawan Beach, and on four nights at the National Stadium 55,000 people at a time get the real thing.

STB calls it Singapore's largest island-wide fan activation for a recording artist. It's also a lot to keep track of. This guide covers the whole programme: concert dates, every BTS THE CITY activation with its dates and price, a map by neighbourhood and the practical details. Each topic links to a full guide.

## **Why is BTS in Singapore such a big deal?**

Three reasons, and each one would matter on its own.

**It's the full group, back after the hiatus.** This is BTS's first Singapore concert as a seven-member group since the *Love Yourself* show at the National Stadium in January 2019\. Seven years, military service for all seven members, and a fifth studio album, *ARIRANG*, released on 20 March 2026\. For many local ARMY this is the first time they'll see the group in their own city.

**Four nights is a lot.** Singapore got more dates than any other city on the Asia leg of the *ARIRANG* tour. The stadium holds up to 55,000 people for a concert, so that's room for around 200,000 fans across the run, and every night sold out in the June ticket sales.

**The whole city is involved.** BTS THE CITY ARIRANG has already run in Seoul, Las Vegas, Busan, London and New York. Singapore is its sixth host city, its only stop in Southeast Asia and its last stop of 2026\. It's also the longest edition so far, at a full month.

## **BTS THE CITY ARIRANG Singapore: every activation, by venue**

### **Jewel Changi Airport — the welcome**

* **Rain Vortex light and music showcase:** 1–26 December, twice nightly at 8pm and 9pm. **Free.** Watch from any level of the Forest Valley, Levels 1 to 4\. Level 1, right beside the waterfall, gets you inside the sound. Level 4 shows you the whole red column at once.  
* **Welcome Experience and stamp rally:** 1–26 December, 10am–10pm, with a photo spot on Level 4\. **Free.**  
* **Who it's for:** visiting ARMY landing at Changi, families and anyone who wants the full experience without a ticket.

![Illustration of a Singapore river bridge illuminated red](/images/bts-singapore/bridge.webp)

### **Marina Bay and the Singapore River — the Ari Red skyline**

* **Landmark illumination:** 8–22 December, nightly from 7pm to 1am (Gardens by the Bay from 11pm). **Free from the street.**  
* **What lights up (15 sites in total):** the Merlion, Singapore Flyer, Gardens by the Bay, The Fullerton Hotel, Helix Bridge, OUE Bayfront, the Asian Civilisations Museum, Millenia Tower, the Peranakan Museum, and six river bridges: Anderson, Cavenagh, Clemenceau, Coleman, Elgin and Ord.

## **The BTS THE CITY stamp rally: every stamp location**

The stamp rally ties BTS month together. It takes you across the whole island, from Changi to Mandai to Sentosa, it runs on the Weverse app and it's free. You don't need a concert ticket, and most stops don't need any ticket at all.

### **How the stamp rally works**

1. **Download Weverse and sign in.** Open the BTS THE CITY ARIRANG Singapore page and find the stamp rally.  
2. **Visit a stamp location and join in.** Each stamp is tied to an activation: watch the show, take the photo, walk the trail.  
3. **Scan the QR code on site** to collect your digital stamp. Look for it on a standing banner or at the activation's entrance.  
4. **Claim your reward** from the stamp rally page once you've collected enough stamps.

HYBE says it will publish the Singapore reward, and how many stamps you need, in a separate notice. Earlier editions give an idea. In Seoul, five stamps earned a special Weverse badge, and in Las Vegas the reward was a one-month trial of BTS digital membership.

### **The best week to collect every stamp**

Apart from the concerts and ARMY Madang, all ten stamp locations are open at the same time from **16 to 22 December**. That's the only stretch when the Fan Village (16–22 Dec) and New Bahru (from 16 Dec) are both running, while the Feel Alive Zone and the Supertrees show (both until 22 Dec) haven't finished yet. It's also concert week, so expect crowds. Go on weekday mornings where you can.

If you're starting earlier in December, you can collect eight stamps before the 16th (from 12 December, once the Supertrees show starts) and pick up the Fan Village and New Bahru later.

### **Stamp rally tips**

* **Watch the closing times.** The Gallery and Museum stamps close at 7pm, the Feel Alive Zone at 8pm, and the Supertrees show lasts only 15 minutes a night.  
* **Keep your phone charged.** You'll need data and enough battery to scan the QR codes. The Feel Alive Zone has a charging station.  
* **Check before you go.** Opening hours can change, and HYBE's separate stamp rally notice will confirm the final list.

## **A map of BTS month, by neighbourhood**

If you plan by area instead of by date, you'll spend more time at the activations and less time on the MRT.

**1\. Changi (East).** Jewel's Rain Vortex show and the stamp rally. Go between 7.30pm and 9.30pm and you can catch both shows. Good on the day you land or the day you fly out.

**2\. The Civic District and Marina Bay (City).** The National Gallery Art Trail, the Supreme Court Terrace pop-up, the National Museum's Dance Mirror and façade, the Ari Red landmarks and the Supertrees show. This is the busiest part of the map and you can walk all of it. Nearest MRT: City Hall, Esplanade, Bayfront and Promenade.

**3\. Kallang (East-central).** The National Stadium, the Feel Alive Zone and the stadium roof lighting. Nearest MRT: Stadium (Circle Line, closest) and Kallang (East–West Line, about a 15-minute walk).

**4\. Sentosa (South).** The SWIM inflatable, the photo points, Sensoryscape, the Run, the drone show, USS and the Fan Village. Plan a full day. Take the Sentosa Express from VivoCity (HarbourFront MRT).

**5\. Robertson Quay and River Valley (Central).** New Bahru's takeover. It pairs well with the bridge stretch of the Red Trail.

**6\. Mandai (North).** The Exploria takeover. A half-day trip on its own; combine it with the wildlife parks.

## **Practical planning: getting around, staying, eating**

### **Getting to the National Stadium**

Stadium MRT on the Circle Line opens straight into the precinct, but on concert nights it gets very crowded. Kallang on the East–West Line is a flat 15-minute walk and usually moves faster after the show. Before the show, expect crowd-control queues at Stadium station's exits. After the show, expect a long wait, so have your exit plan ready before the encore.

### **Where to stay**

Stay in the **city centre** (Bugis, City Hall, Marina) if you want a short ride to Kallang and the Red Trail outside your door. Stay at **Kallang or Tanjong Rhu** if you want to walk home after the concert. Stay on **Sentosa** if you're coming with family and your days are about USS and the beach. 

### **Dressing for 31 degrees and a stadium**

Choose breathable fabrics, shoes that can handle stadium stairs, and something in Ari Red if you want to match the city.

**Visiting from overseas**

Fill in the SG Arrival Card before you fly. A credit card or a SimplyGo EZ-Link card works on every MRT gate (and the BTS edition is a good souvenir). December is wet, with afternoon storms, so pack a compact umbrella. If you're coming from Johor Bahru, expect the Causeway to be jammed on concert days.

## **Frequently asked questions**

**When is the BTS concert in Singapore?** BTS WORLD TOUR 'ARIRANG' plays the National Stadium on Thursday 17, Saturday 19, Sunday 20 and Tuesday 22 December 2026\. Shows start at 7pm.

**Are BTS Singapore tickets still available?** No. All four nights are sold out. Watch Ticketmaster and Live Nation Singapore for any official release, and don't buy from social media resellers.

**What is BTS THE CITY ARIRANG Singapore?** It's a month-long takeover of the city by HYBE and the Singapore Tourism Board, running from 1 to 31 December 2026\. It includes light shows, installations, an art trail, a fun run, a drone show and themed experiences across Singapore. Most of it is free.

**When will Singapore's landmarks turn red for BTS?** Every night from 8 to 22 December, from 7pm to 1am; Gardens by the Bay begins at 11pm. There are 15 sites in total, including the Merlion, Singapore Flyer, Gardens by the Bay, the Helix Bridge and six bridges along the Singapore River.

**What time is the BTS light show at Jewel?** At 8pm and 9pm daily from 1 to 26 December, at the Rain Vortex. It's free.

**How does the BTS THE CITY stamp rally work in Singapore?** Join on the Weverse app, visit any of the 12 stamp locations, take part in the activation and scan the on-site QR code to collect a digital stamp. Most stamps are free and don't need a concert ticket. HYBE will announce the reward and the number of stamps needed in a separate notice.

**Can I experience BTS in Singapore without a concert ticket?** Yes. Jewel, the red landmarks, the Supertrees show, the National Museum installations, the Feel Alive Zone at The Kallang and Sentosa's installations are all free.
