A lip-colour pot, a tint with an applicator and a bottle of toner ask different things of a bathroom shelf. Korean beauty is a broad category; choosing a label is only the start of choosing a product.

This guide brings together eight brands through specific entries in their official catalogues. The selection spans colour cosmetics, a toner, an ampoule and a cleanser. It is a research-based introduction, not a ranking of results: no independent wear, humidity or clinical testing was conducted for this article.

## How to use this guide

Begin with a gap in your routine. Compare the format, shade or ingredient list that matters to you, then check the exact product sold in your market. A claim on a brand's page is the manufacturer's description; it does not establish how the product will perform on your skin.

For readers in Singapore, Kuala Lumpur or Jakarta, delivery, local stock, returns and the full landed price still need checking with the seller. The linked pages are research sources, not confirmation of local availability. Prices and stock are deliberately omitted where no local check has been made.

## 1. Fwee: a pot for lip and cheek colour

Fwee's [Blurry Pudding Pot](https://fwee.us/products/pudding-pot) is presented as a lip and cheek product. The official page provides shade names and swatches, making it a useful starting point if you want to compare one colour across two uses.

Its inclusion here is about that format. The previous version of this article treated Fwee broadly as a skincare-and-makeup hybrid with specific barrier-care ingredients. That generalisation was not supported by the references provided. Read the ingredient list for the precise product and shade you intend to buy.

Compare the application method with a tint you already own. A pot may suit someone who enjoys blending colour with a brush or fingertip; it may be less convenient for another person's on-the-go routine. That is a practical preference, not a performance ranking.

## 2. Hince: a radiance balm to examine in person

Hince's official catalogue lists [True Dimension Radiance Balm](https://hince.co.kr/product/detail.html?product_no=776). It gives this entry a concrete focus: the finish and format of one product, rather than an assumption that the entire brand suits a particular climate.

When comparing a balm, look at where the manufacturer recommends applying it and the finish shown in daylight swatches. A photograph cannot tell you how it will layer over your own base. If possible, inspect the shade in person or consult the seller's returns policy before ordering.

## 3. Unleashia: eye colour with a clear point of comparison

[Unleashia's catalogue](https://unleashiacosmetics.com/) includes the Glitterpedia Eye Palette alongside other eye, lip and complexion products. The palette offers a starting point for readers comparing eye-colour formats and finishes.

Choose the exact palette before comparing swatches: similarly named collections can contain different colours and textures. Follow the product's application instructions. The presence of glitter in a collection is not a reason to assume every formula has the same intended use.

## 4. Rom&nd: compare the tint, then the shade

Rom&nd lists [Juicy Lasting Tint](https://www.romandglobal.com/collections/juicy) in its colour range. It is a useful counterpart to Fwee's pot format and Peripera's velvet tint when deciding how you prefer to apply lip colour.

Start with your own lip colour and the depth you want, rather than expecting a swatch to look identical on everyone. Compare photographs across different lighting where available. For complexion products, our [foundation shade guide](/beauty/the-ultimate-guide-to-finding-the-perfect-foundation-shade-for-southeast-asian-skin-tones/) considers the separate question of undertone.

## 5. Anua: read beyond the ingredient in the name

Anua's [Heartleaf 77 Soothing Toner](https://anua.com/products/heartleaf-77-soothing-toner) is one specific product to research. Its name and positioning do not establish that every Anua formula shares the same ingredients or suits the same person.

Consult the full ingredient list and directions on the version available to you. This guide does not independently substantiate anti-inflammatory effects or promise that the toner will calm reactive skin. If your current routine already meets your needs, a prominent ingredient is not itself a reason to add another step.

## 6. Peripera: another lip-colour format

CLUB CLIO's official store lists [Peripera Ink The Velvet](https://clubclio.shop/products/peripera-ink-the-velvet). Here it serves as a specific option for readers comparing lip-colour formats, without the earlier blanket endorsement of the brand's performance or shade suitability.

Look at the exact shade number as well as its name. Collections, packaging and stock can change between markets. Keep the shade and product details together when comparing sellers, rather than ordering from a photograph alone.

## 7. SKIN1004: distinguish the ampoule from the rest of the range

[Madagascar Centella Ampoule](https://www.skin1004.com/products/skin1004-madagascar-centella-ampoule) is listed on SKIN1004's official site. It is included as an example of the ampoule format; the brand's own product information is the starting source.

An ingredient associated with a brand does not make all of its products interchangeable. Check the complete formula of the ampoule rather than transferring claims from a cleanser or sunscreen in the same range. No claim of universal suitability for sensitive or acne-prone skin is made here.

## 8. Arencia: assess the cleanser as a cleanser

Arencia lists [Fresh Green Rice Mochi Cleanser](https://arencia.us/products/arencia-fresh-green-mochi-cleanser) in its catalogue. Its named format gives readers a specific product to examine instead of a general claim about handcrafted skincare.

Read the directions, ingredients and quantity before comparing it with your usual cleanser. A botanical description or unusual texture cannot establish that a formula will be non-irritating. This article does not independently verify the manufacturer's efficacy claims or assess its production process.

## Before placing an order

Check the seller's identity, product version, quantity, delivery terms and returns policy. Compare like with like: a discounted mini and a full-size product can appear similar in a listing. Ingredient lists and directions on the actual pack should guide use.
