## Start with the product you need

K-beauty covers skincare and colour cosmetics. The label alone cannot tell you which formula, finish or shade suits you. Begin with the eight-brand guide below: each entry links to a specific official product listing and explains what remains untested.

## Read the evidence behind a recommendation

Brand pages establish product names and manufacturer descriptions. Independent testing would be needed to assess wear in tropical humidity or substantiate a comparative performance claim. Our guide states its research method so you can judge the recommendation on that basis.

## Compare across the region

For the wider retail picture, read the [C-beauty topic guide](/topics/c-beauty/). For more reporting on skincare and colour, browse the [Beauty edit](/beauty/). Check the version, seller and delivery terms available in your own market before buying.
