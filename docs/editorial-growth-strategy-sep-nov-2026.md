# Arahkaii editorial growth strategy — September to November 2026

Prepared 3 September 2026. This plan assumes one flagship and one shorter,
verified dispatch each week. A slot moves rather than publishing without the
reporting, rights, or expert review promised by the commission.

## The publication Arahkaii can own

Arahkaii should become the Asia-first, Muslim-conscious modern-luxury
publication that makes beautiful coverage genuinely useful. Its moat is not a
generic luxury tone. It is the combination of:

- tropical and regional reality;
- modest style without treating Muslim women as a trend category;
- dated halal and hospitality verification;
- prayer-aware routes and city intelligence;
- first-hand tests, visits, interviews, original photography, and small data
  products;
- culturally fluent judgment about Southeast Asian makers and places.

The first three authority lanes are:

1. Considered style in tropical Asia.
2. Arahkaii Verified dining and hospitality.
3. Prayer-aware city life and travel.

C-beauty remains a test-led supporting lane. It expands only when products,
experts, retail availability, or original data can be documented.

## Editorial products worth building

### The Arahkaii Index

A recurring, downloadable dataset: regional designers, modest workwear,
restaurant status and prices, hotel prayer amenities, or beauty availability.
Publish the fields, method, verification date, corrections path, and CSV where
appropriate. This is the strongest citation and digital-PR opportunity.

### Arahkaii Verified

Every venue entry shows what was checked, by whom, when, using which official
source, and what the status does and does not mean. Add price band, occasion,
booking friction, accessibility, and a concise editorial verdict.

### The Tropical Test

Repeatable first-hand testing for fabric, construction, makeup wear, comfort,
and travel gear in Southeast Asian conditions. Publish conditions, duration,
comparison criteria, products supplied or purchased, and limitations.

### The Considered City

Route-tested city guides built around neighbourhood texture, transport,
prayer-aware timing, halal dining, design, and realistic pacing—not generic
lists of attractions.

### The Studio Visit

Profiles anchored in a real interview or visit. Focus on a material, decision,
business tension, or cultural practice rather than a promotional biography.

## Weekly operating rhythm

- Monday: close evidence gaps, fact-check, rights check, link map, feature-image
  master, headline and standfirst.
- Tuesday: publish flagship; request only normal indexing; send newsletter lead;
  schedule native social derivatives.
- Wednesday: create reciprocal internal links, pitch one relevant journalist or
  expert, and capture reader questions.
- Thursday: verify the dispatch and prepare its lighter visual package.
- Friday: publish dispatch; share the most useful new fact, not a duplicate
  flagship summary.
- End of week: record indexation, engagement, saves, recirculation, newsletter
  conversion, corrections, and reporting lessons.

## Inoreader research desk

Inoreader is the monitored-source intake layer. It finds changes and leads;
it does not write, verify, or approve stories.

Reuse the newsroom structure already present in the account:

- `Arahkaii Topic Intelligence` for broad discovery and commissioning leads;
- `AK 01 Editorial & Culture` for reported culture, people and place leads;
- `AK 02 Visual Trends` for image, design and format signals;
- `HH 01 Official & Certification` as a shared primary-source watchlist when a
  halal claim is relevant to Arahkaii;
- `HH 04 Travel & Prayer-Friendly` as a shared source pool for prayer-aware
  travel reporting.

Do not copy every Humble Halal item into Arahkaii. A shared item enters an
Arahkaii commission only when it fits the publication's Asia-first lifestyle
brief and can support distinct reporting. Keep the existing narrow monitoring
feeds, including the Singapore design-trends feed, but audit their queries and
unread volume monthly for noise.

Use these tags consistently:

- `source-primary`
- `source-expert`
- `lead-not-evidence`
- `needs-original-source`
- `needs-local-reporting`
- `commission-candidate`
- `fact-change`
- `rights-lead`
- `hold`

The account currently uses 26 of its 30 rule slots. Preserve the four remaining
slots rather than creating broad new rules. Reuse and tighten the active
`Arahkaii — Editorial & Culture` and `Arahkaii — Visual Trends` rules; apply the
source-class and commissioning tags manually until a repeated, high-volume
pattern earns automation. Suppress deal, coupon, affiliate and
duplicate-release noise at the feed or bundle level where possible. Monitoring
feeds should remain narrow and regional. Track changes on certification, menu,
price, opening-hour and hotel-amenity pages, but verify the original page before
Arahkaii changes a claim.

The editorial handoff is:

`Inoreader lead → original source → claim ledger → reporting brief → human
reporting → article PR → social package → Blotato approval and schedule`

Never cite an Inoreader summary, AI summary or proxy URL as evidence.

## Blotato distribution plan

Blotato should schedule native versions from an approved source package; it
should not turn one caption into identical posts everywhere.

For each Tuesday flagship:

- Day 0: Instagram 6–8-slide 4:5 carousel—proposition, evidence, nuance, and
  canonical call to read.
- Day 1: Pinterest 2:3 guide pin with a searchable, accurate promise.
- Day 2 or 3: 20–35 second vertical video showing the test, route, object,
  location, or method; use a conceptual visual only with disclosure.
- Day 4: Threads field note or sourced quotation with enough context to stand
  alone.
- Day 7: second Pinterest asset built around a table, route, material, price, or
  checklist readers may save.

Every package must retain the canonical URL, claim map, exact excerpts used,
alt text, rights record, disclosure, account target, scheduled time, content
hash, dry-run state, and human approval.

## Feature-image production standard

Create a separate clean master for article, schema and Discover:

- 2400×1350 preferred, 16:9, no logo or baked-in headline;
- documentary or accurately labelled conceptual status;
- one focal subject, natural detail, crop-safe negative space;
- descriptive filename, contextual alt text, caption, credit, and rights record;
- responsive derivatives with explicit dimensions and only the true hero
  prioritized for loading.

Then create social derivatives at 1080×1350, 1080×1920, and 1000×1500. The
supplied “Quiet Luxury” and “Considered Style” portraits are strong directional
social covers but should be remade at platform dimensions and not used as the
article OG image. “Tactile Southeast Asian fashion discovery” is a strong
carousel concept; it needs a 4:5 derivative and factually identified designers
or materials before publication. The two 1536×1024 boards are production and
brand references, not reader-facing story art.

## Site improvements that support a real publisher

1. Add a visible methodology/verification module to tests, guides and ranked
   lists.
2. Add “last verified,” corrections and material-update trails near the byline.
3. Turn topic hubs into editorial dossiers with a pillar, latest reporting,
   dataset/download, and named editors—not tag archives.
4. Add save, print and newsletter actions to high-utility guides while keeping
   ads visually separate from editorial controls.
5. Launch contributor commissioning standards and pay/rights terms before
   expanding regional coverage.
6. Build a media kit around verified audience geography, engagement,
   newsletter conversion and flagship work; never use projected reach as fact.
7. Add a monthly newsroom page that lists new reporting, corrections, index
   releases and verification updates.
8. Improve mobile LCP after the current AdSense review by testing deferred ad
   loading and reducing third-party main-thread work without compromising
   consent.

## Eve decision

Do not install Eve inside the public Astro site during this phase. Ranking,
AdSense review, article rendering and the Inoreader-to-Blotato workflow do not
require an agent runtime. Eve is also currently beta, so combining it with the
production publication would add dependencies and operational risk without an
immediate reader benefit.

Reconsider a separate `arahkaii-newsroom-agent` project after four to six weeks
of manual use, when the real repetitive steps are measurable. A useful first
version would be read-only by default and would:

- turn approved Inoreader items into evidence-gap briefs;
- audit verification age, missing credits and internal-link opportunities;
- draft GitHub pull requests without merging them;
- prepare Blotato packages with publishing approval required;
- run evals against the anti-slop and claim-ledger gates.

Keep the agent in its own repository and Vercel project. It should never write
directly to production, treat summaries as sources, invent reporting, or post
to Blotato without a durable human approval.

## Success gates after 90 days

- At least three proprietary assets with published methods.
- At least 75% of scheduled indexable stories contain two originality units.
- No newly indexed article lacks author accountability, evidence, rights, or a
  useful inbound internal link.
- Stronger non-brand query breadth and engaged organic sessions at the cluster
  level.
- Measurable newsletter sign-ups, return visits, saves and recirculation from
  the new formats.
- Earned references to an Arahkaii index, test, verification, or original
  interview.
- A conscious decision to expand, hold or close each active cluster based on
  evidence rather than publishing volume.
