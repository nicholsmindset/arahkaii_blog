# Arahkaii eight-week editorial plan

This schedule builds consistent topical depth without publishing filler. Every story must meet the source and method requirements enforced by `npm run validate:content`; postpone a slot rather than publish an unverified draft.

## Publishing rhythm

- Tuesday: reported or expert-led service story.
- Thursday: supporting search story that answers a narrow reader question.
- Sunday: Arahkaii Weekly, linking only to work published or substantively updated that week.
- Optional third web story: use only when original reporting, first-hand testing or a meaningful update is ready.

## Weeks 1–2: halal dining in Singapore

- Re-verify the 2026 fine-dining guide directly with each venue and MUIS where applicable.
- Publish a narrow explainer on Muslim-friendly versus halal-certified restaurant claims in Singapore.
- Add one original interview with a chef, restaurateur or halal-assurance specialist.
- Refresh the pillar and reciprocal internal links after each supporting story ships.

## Weeks 3–4: C-beauty in Southeast Asia

- Publish a tested guide to where readers can buy leading C-beauty brands through authorised channels.
- Add an ingredient or formulation explainer reviewed by a qualified cosmetic scientist or dermatologist.
- Conduct one founder, retailer or makeup-artist interview focused on Southeast Asian skin tones and climate.
- Reassess the temporarily noindexed overlapping C-beauty drafts; merge useful original material into the pillar before retiring or republishing them.

## Weeks 5–6: modest fashion and investment dressing

- Publish a construction-led guide based on first-hand garment review or named expert interviews.
- Cover one Southeast Asian designer with original reporting and current price/stock verification.
- Build a practical care-and-cost-per-wear companion to the investment-dressing pillar.
- Consolidate overlapping Korean-fashion and heritage-brand drafts unless each can serve a distinct search intent with original evidence.

## Weeks 7–8: prayer-aware travel and considered stays

- Publish one city guide with first-hand or directly verified prayer-space, transport and dining information.
- Add a hotel-review methodology page covering visit status, hosted stays, update dates and corrections.
- Publish one narrow planning guide based on official tourism, transport and prayer-facility sources.
- Review the travel pillar's links, freshness dates and claims after the new supporting pieces are live.

## Evidence and quality gate

Before publication, confirm:

- A distinct reader question and search intent, with no material overlap with an indexed page.
- At least one substantive primary or authoritative source, plus transparent first-hand/reporting methodology where applicable.
- Named author, appropriate reviewer, publish/update dates, original image rights and useful alternative text.
- Clear commercial disclosure for hosted, gifted, affiliate or sponsored involvement.
- Links to the relevant pillar and at least one closely related supporting story.
- A considered headline and standfirst that accurately describe the page; no traffic claims or superlatives without evidence.

## Eight-week review

Use Search Console and GA4 to compare indexed pages, non-brand impressions, qualified organic sessions, article recirculation, 50/90 percent scroll events and newsletter conversions. Keep successful clusters active; consolidate pages that still compete for the same query or attract no qualified readership after they have had a fair indexing period.
