# Commission: refresh the Singapore halal fine-dining pillar

Status: reporting required before material article changes  
Owner: Arahkaii editorial  
Canonical page: `/dining/halal-fine-dining-singapore-2026/`  
Target window: 29 September–2 October 2026

## Reader job

Help a Muslim diner choose an appropriate special-occasion restaurant in
Singapore while understanding the exact certification or operating status,
current price, booking conditions, alcohol policy, location and editorial
trade-offs.

## Why this specialist refresh matters

The page is Arahkaii's pillar for the Singapore dining cluster and already
appears in Google. It has a useful status distinction and a declared desk-
research method, but the current prose and evidence record do not yet meet the
new Arahkaii publishing standard.

This is one specialist service assignment within a broad modern-Asian luxury
and culture publication. It must not determine the balance or identity of the
overall commissioning calendar.

## Issues to resolve

1. The method says the article is desk researched, while the opening and
   several passages use sensory detail that can imply a visit. Retain such
   detail only where visit notes or attributable sources support it.
2. Twelve venues are listed, but the frontmatter records only four general
   sources. Each venue needs a current official status and operating source.
3. “Best” and ranked/editorial verdict language needs a visible selection
   method and comparable fields.
4. The article's `lastVerified` date is 25 May while its updated date is 2
   September. Every status-sensitive entry needs a dated verification record.
5. Rempapa is explicitly non-halal and cooks pork. It should not occupy a ranked
   position in a halal recommendation list. If editorial context is valuable,
   place it outside the recommendations in a clearly labelled context note.
6. “Muslim-owned,” “pork-free,” alcohol policy and MUIS certification must
   remain distinct. Never infer one status from another.
7. Prices, menu format, awards, chef biographies, “first” claims and booking
   advice require current primary or authoritative support.
8. The feature-image caption is empty and the existing image may be text-heavy.
   The page needs a rights-cleared, clean 16:9 master suitable for article,
   schema and Discover.
9. Some links leave the reader's dining task for tangential fashion/luxury
   pages. Replace them with the dining cluster, verified-method page, brunch
   guide when ready, Singapore route guide and a relevant subscription action.

## Required originality units

The refreshed page needs both:

- a reproducible, time-stamped venue/status/price dataset; and
- first-hand notes and original or rights-cleared photographs from at least two
  recommended venues.

If visits cannot be completed, the page may be improved as a transparent
verified directory, but it must not read as an anonymous review or imply a
visit.

## Verification procedure

For each venue:

1. Search the current MUIS-certified establishment directory and record the
   result and check date.
2. Open the official venue site or booking page and record menu, current price,
   service days, address and booking URL.
3. Obtain direct confirmation for Muslim-owned, pork-free, cross-contamination
   or alcohol-policy claims that MUIS certification does not answer.
4. Verify awards and rankings at the issuing publication or organization.
5. Record whether Arahkaii visited, who visited, visit date, what was purchased,
   any hosted/gifted relationship and the available image rights.
6. Narrow or remove claims that cannot be supported.

Use `docs/commissioning/halal-fine-dining-verification.csv` as the working
ledger. Do not place personal contact details, credentials or private notes in
the repository.

## Selection method to publish

Score only venues eligible for recommendation using consistently reported
dimensions:

- exact halal/certification status and clarity;
- food and culinary argument, supported by visit or attributable evidence;
- special-occasion room and service;
- value within its format;
- booking practicality and location;
- distinctiveness within Singapore's dining landscape.

Do not convert the dimensions into a fake mathematical precision. Explain the
editorial judgement and identify who the recommendation is for.

## Feature-image brief

- Clean 2400×1350 documentary image from an included venue, with written rights
  for editorial website and social use.
- No logo, title or other text in the article/OG/schema master.
- Natural warm light, one plated course or dining-room focal point, accurate
  colour and enough crop-safe space for 16:9 and 4:5 derivatives.
- Record descriptive filename, visible-fact alt text, venue/context caption,
  photographer or supplied-image credit and rights source.
- If no documentary rights are available, use a restrained conceptual table
  study and label it as an Arahkaii conceptual illustration. Do not associate
  it visually with a named venue or dish.
