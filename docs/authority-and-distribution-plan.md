# Arahkaii authority and distribution plan

Ahrefs' 2 September 2026 baseline shows Domain Rating 0.4 and no detected ranking keywords or estimated Singapore organic traffic. Treat the next phase as authority building from a near-zero baseline; avoid buying links or publishing generic volume content.

## Publisher-ready foundation

- Complete every author biography with verifiable experience, geography, specialist beats and links to published work. Do not invent credentials.
- Add an explicit visit/test/interview status to reviews and recommendations, and disclose hosted, gifted, affiliate or sponsored involvement.
- Keep original reporting materials, permission records and source notes with each story.
- Maintain a corrections log and update high-intent guides whenever facts materially change.
- Prepare a compact media kit only after GA4 and Search Console have collected continuous, defensible data.

## Earned-authority programme

Each month, target a small number of relevant, editorially earned mentions:

- Pitch data-led or reported findings to Singapore and Southeast Asian fashion, beauty, dining and travel writers.
- Offer named Arahkaii editors as sources only within their documented expertise.
- Ask interviewed experts, venues and brands to reference the published piece where genuinely useful; never require or purchase a link.
- Create one link-worthy primary asset per focus cluster: a transparent dataset, original survey, expert round-up or regularly maintained directory.
- Reclaim only real unlinked brand mentions and broken links identified through monitoring.

## Distribution loop for every article

1. Publish the article with its cluster links, sources, method and disclosure complete.
2. Submit or inspect the URL in Search Console; do not repeatedly request indexing.
3. Feature it once in Arahkaii Weekly with a distinct editorial note.
4. Adapt the strongest insight for owned social channels and link to the canonical article.
5. Send a concise, personalised note to sources or relevant editors when the piece materially serves their audience.
6. Revisit after 28 and 56 days using impressions, query fit, engaged sessions, recirculation and subscriptions—not raw pageviews alone.

## Partnership and syndication guardrails

- For Tatler Asia or another established publisher, lead with original access, regional expertise and a clean evidence trail rather than audience-size claims.
- Agree in writing which party owns the work and which URL is canonical before syndication.
- Use `rel="sponsored"` for paid placements and clearly label commercial relationships for readers.
- Do not republish the same article across domains without canonical or licensing terms.
- Keep editorial decisions independent of advertiser approval and document conflicts of interest.

## Initial outreach assets

- A one-page publication profile: mission, beats, locations covered, editor biographies and editorial standards.
- Three flagship story links demonstrating original reporting or transparent expert-led service journalism.
- A short pitch template focused on the recipient's readers and the specific evidence Arahkaii can contribute.
- A source/contact sheet organised by cluster, with permission and last-contact dates.
- A monthly record of pitches, responses, earned mentions and referring domains.

## Success measures for the first 90 days

- Valid indexed pages and impressions grow without indexed thin/tag archives returning.
- At least three focus clusters receive new original reporting and reciprocal internal links.
- Relevant referring domains and unbranded search impressions begin to appear.
- Newsletter conversion and article recirculation establish a measurable baseline.
- No undisclosed commercial content, unsupported superlatives or outdated verification claims are published.
