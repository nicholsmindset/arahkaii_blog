> One hero (16:9) + one inline variant for every calendar topic in `content-calendar.md`.
> Built from `scripts/image-prompt.mjs` + the locked `image-system.md` system. **Assemble
> a final prompt as:** `PREFIX` + the topic's **Hero** (or **Inline**) line + `TAIL`.
> Rules unchanged: official-site sourcing first for real named venues; **never commit a
> sourced image without approval + a logged licence**; generate when rights are unclear;
> modest dress always; enforce the §5 never-images list (no alcohol/bar/nightlife, no neon,
> no stock clichés). Palette locked to paper #F4F0E7 · taupe · clay · sage · gold · ink.

**PREFIX (prepend to every prompt):**
> *Editorial photograph for a luxury Asian publication (Tatler Asia / Vogue Arabia register).*

**TAIL (append to every prompt):**
> *Soft natural window light from camera-left, warm 4200K, muted slightly-desaturated grade with warm shadows and ivory highlights, locked palette (paper, taupe, clay, sage, gold). Modest dress — no exposed shoulders/knees; no alcohol/bar/nightlife; no text, logos or watermarks. Shot on medium-format film (Portra 400), film grain, natural skin texture with pores, asymmetrical features, anatomically correct hands with no extra fingers, no AI sheen, no plastic gloss, no HDR, no stock-photo composition.*

**HERO rule:** primary subject **centred in the safe zone (middle ~60%)**, generous even negative space, narrow depth of field, **16:9**. **INLINE rule:** rule-of-thirds, negative space to one side, ratio as noted (3:2 default).
