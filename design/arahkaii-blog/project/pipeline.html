Editorial Standards

How we publish

A small publication, made by hand — and read, every word, before it reaches you.

We believe a reader can feel the difference between something made carefully and something made quickly. So we have built our newsroom around a single, unbreakable rule: nothing is published until a person has read it, weighed it, and stood behind it.

Here is how a story travels from an idea to the page.

01

It begins with an editor

Every story starts with a person and a point of view — an angle worth your time, and a reason it belongs in Arahkaii.

02

We research in the open

Facts, quotes and images are gathered and traced back to their source. If we can’t credit it, we don’t run it.

03

We draft with care

We use modern tools to help shape and structure a draft — the way any newsroom uses the tools of its time. They assist the work; they never replace the judgement behind it.

The human review gate

Nothing reaches you unread

An editor reads, fact-checks and approves every word, image and credit before a story is published. This step is never skipped, and never automated. It is the whole promise of Arahkaii.

04

We publish slowly

Once approved, a story is set, proofed and laid out by hand. We would rather be late and right than early and loud.

05

We stand by it

Corrections are made openly and dated. Sources stay cited. Your trust is not a by-product of the work — it is the work.

Our commitments
Human oversight
A named editor approves every published story.
Sourced & credited
Every fact, quote and image is attributed to its origin.
No dark patterns
No autoplay, no infinite feed, no manufactured urgency.
Open corrections
Mistakes are fixed in public, and dated.
Reader first
We measure success in trust, not in clicks.

The most luxurious thing a publication can do is take its time. So we do.
