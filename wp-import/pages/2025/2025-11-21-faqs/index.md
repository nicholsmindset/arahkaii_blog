**1\. What is Arahkaii.com?** Arahkaii is a Muslim-owned, Asian modern-luxury publication built on eight editorial pillars: Style, Beauty, Dining, Travel, Living, People, Culture, and Guides. We cover what discerning readers across Asia actually want to read — from Seoul runways to Singapore's halal-certified fine dining to the founders quietly reshaping Asian luxury.

**2\. Who is Arahkaii for?** Readers who want modest-luxury treated as a primary editorial position, not a footnote. Our audience expects halal status declared plainly in Dining, Travel, and Guides coverage, and they expect the cultural intelligence to know why Songzio's Paris show matters and why a $3 tote can outsell a $3,000 handbag.

**3\. What are the eight editorial pillars?**

- **Style** — Modest-luxury fashion through an Asian lens.
- **Beauty** — Asian skin, Asian science, halal-aware formulation.
- **Dining** — Halal-certified, Muslim-owned, alcohol-free — declared plainly.
- **Travel** — Halal-aware destination guides at locals' granularity.
- **Living** — Home, energy, and conscious-luxury at daily scale.
- **People** — Founders, designers, and the next generation of Asian creative directors.
- **Culture** — The ideas, money, and movements shaping Asian luxury.
- **Guides** — Cornerstone reference content built to be bookmarked.

**4\. Does Arahkaii cover alcohol, bars, or nightlife?** No. We do not produce alcohol, bar, nightclub, wine, beer, cocktail, champagne, whisky, gin, rum, or cognac content. Dining and Travel pieces declare halal status (halal-certified, Muslim-owned, alcohol-free, or alcohol in cooking) plainly in the body of the article.

**5\. Who produces the content?** Our in-house editorial team collaborates with industry experts, brand founders, and contributors who understand the modest-luxury reader. Every piece passes a halal-alignment and anti-AI-slop checklist before publication.

**6\. Can brands or creators contribute?** Yes, for features, founder profiles, and special projects — provided they align with our editorial values: craft, ethics, halal-awareness where relevant, and Asia-rooted storytelling. Reach us at hello@arahkaii.com.

**7\. How often is new content published?** We publish weekly across the eight pillars, with the Arahkaii Evening Edit and occasional Editor's Letters threaded throughout.

**8\. Does Arahkaii feature local Singapore brands?** Yes. Singapore and Southeast Asia are central to our coverage, alongside Korea, Japan, Indonesia, Malaysia, and the broader Asian luxury landscape.

**9\. Is access to Arahkaii.com free?** All editorial content is freely accessible worldwide without subscription fees.

**10\. How do you select brands and topics?** By three filters: quality (does it last and is it made with care), ethics (how was it made and by whom), and cultural fit (does it speak to the Asian modern-luxury reader). Halal status is a standing requirement for Dining, Travel, and Guides recommendations.

**11\. Can readers subscribe for updates?** Yes — subscribe to our newsletter for new features, the Evening Edit, and pillar-specific deep dives.

**12\. Does Arahkaii offer advertising or sponsored content?** Yes, within our editorial guidelines. We accept brand partnerships, sponsored features, and advertising that align with our pillars and audience. We do not run alcohol or nightlife advertising. See our Advertise page for details.

**13\. How does Arahkaii ensure editorial independence?** While collaborating with brands for content, we maintain strict editorial guidelines. Sponsored content is labelled. Brand inclusions never override our halal and quality standards.

**14\. Are influencer partnerships available?** Yes, for ambassadors and collaborators whose audience and aesthetic match ours.

**15\. How is Arahkaii relevant to the Singapore luxury market?** We were built in Singapore, for the Asian modern-luxury reader — with a halal-aware filter that the established luxury press has historically treated as optional. That filter is our position, not our compromise.

**16\. How can I contact the team?** Email hello@arahkaii.com, or use our contact form. We aim to respond within 48 business hours.

**17\. Is user data collected?** Yes, in compliance with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), to enhance your reading experience.

**18\. Can I share articles?** Yes — share freely via social media and direct links.

**19\. How does Arahkaii support sustainability?** We prioritize brands championing sustainable, ethical, and regenerative practices — surfacing them across Style, Beauty, Living, and Culture coverage.

**20\. Does Arahkaii cover events and shopping guides?** Yes — Style covers fashion weeks (Seoul, NYFW, LFW, MFW, Paris), Beauty tracks ingredient launches, Dining and Travel run city guides, and Guides hosts our cornerstone buying references.
